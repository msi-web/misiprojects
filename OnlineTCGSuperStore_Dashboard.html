Online Trading Card Store - Sample Dashboard

Portfolio Sample

A simple Power BI dashboard built for a fictional online trading card game (TCG) business, turning full-year 2024 sales data into trends that guide smarter, forward-looking decisions.

Sample ProjectFor Demonstration Purposes

The data used in this project was AI-generated based on prompts I created, intended to showcase example dashboard design and analysis.

The Business Question

The online TCG store's owner had no clear way to see which products, months, or customer groups were actually driving revenue — and needed a single view of 2024 sales performance to guide smarter decisions for the year ahead.

The Data Source

A fictional, generated dataset covering full-year 2024 online sales, including orders, product categories, customer demographics, and state-level sales data. Created using Perplexity.ai

My Approach

I modeled the data in Power Query Editor (via Power BI), connecting product, customer, and order tables, then built DAX measures to surface revenue trends by month, product, region, and age group. 

Slicers and a map visual let the owner explore performance from multiple angles without digging through raw spreadsheets.

Visualizations

Key Findings

Total 2024 sales reached $279,000 across 2,000 orders, averaging $157 in revenue per customer.

August was the strongest month, generating $28,000 in sales driven by major Pokémon and Star Wars Unlimited releases.

Oklahoma, Maine, and Florida produced the highest order volumes.

Customers under 25 and those aged 45–54 drove the most revenue ($52K) compared to other age groups.

Pokémon products led all sales at $134,000, followed by Star Wars Unlimited at $94,000.
