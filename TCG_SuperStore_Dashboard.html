Trading Card Super Store - Sample Dashboard

Portfolio Sample

A simple Power BI dashboard built for a fictional trading card game (TCG) business, turning 2024–mid 2026 sales, cost, and budget data into a clear view of store performance and profitability.

Sample ProjectFor Demonstration Purposes

The data used in this project was AI-generated based on prompts I created, intended to showcase example dashboard design and analysis.

The Business Question

A TCG (Trading Card Game) Super Store's owner had no centralized way to see how the business was really performing — and wanted one place to view revenue, profitability, product performance, and progress toward budgeted goals to finally understand the true state of his business.

The Data Source

A fictional, generated dataset spanning 12 related tables covering sales transactions, products, stores, expenses, and budget targets from 2024 through mid-2026. Created my me leveraging Perplexity.ai

My Approach

I built the data model in Power Query Editor (via Power BI), connecting and cleaning 12 tables, then used DAX to create calculated measures and columns for revenue, margin, and budget-variance tracking. 

Interactive slicers and custom visuals let a business owner drill into performance by store, product, and time period.

Key Findings

Total revenue reached $6.8M across 280,000 orders between 2024 and March 2026, with trading cards driving $6.0M of that total.

Gross profit hit $2.97M — 92% of target, falling short by $246K.

Pokémon Booster Packs were the top revenue driver at $2.3M, more than double Star Wars Booster Packs at $983K.

Operating expenses totaled $1.38M, or 20% of total revenue.
