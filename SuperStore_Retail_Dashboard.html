Super Store - Sample Dashboard

Portfolio Sample

A Power BI dashboard built for a fictional stakeholder at Super Store, a major online/retail business, turning high-level insights into targeted marketing opportunities that can drive revenue growth.

Sample ProjectFor Demonstration Purposes

The data used in this project was AI-generated based on prompts I created, intended to showcase example dashboard design and analysis.

The Business Question

Super Store's stakeholder had no clear way to see which states, products, or customer types were actually driving revenue, and needed a high-level view of sales data to identify where targeted marketing campaigns could have the biggest impact.

The Data Source

A fictional retail sales dataset sourced from Kaggle, covering yearly sales performance across states, product categories, and customer segments.

My Approach

I built the data model in Power Query, created calculated measures and columns using DAX, and added slicers to let a stakeholder explore sales by state, product, and customer type.

Key Findings

Average yearly sales totaled $565K, with California and New York generating the most revenue.

Phones and Chairs consistently outsold other product categories, bringing in $70K to $100K annually.

Consumer customers spent over $100K per year, significantly outpacing both Corporate and Home Office segments.
