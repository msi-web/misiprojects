Super Trading Card Store Dashboard

Microsoft Power BI Dashboard Overview

This dashboard was designed for a fictional trading card game (TCG) business owner to track key business metrics from 2024 through mid-2026. It provides insight into store performance, product sales, profitability based on supplier costs, and performance against budgeted goals.

This dashboard focuses on a more complex design to showcase actionable results, all based on a large generated data set with 12 tables.

Click here to view the Power Point presentation (PDF)

Key insights:

  • Total revenue (2024 – March 2026) was $6.8M across 280,000 orders, with trading card product sales totaling $6.0M.
  • Gross profit was $2.97M, reaching 92% of the target and falling short by $246K.
  • Pokémon Booster Packs generated the highest revenue at $2.3M, compared with $983K for Star Wars Booster Packs.
  • Operating expenses totaled $1.38M, representing 20% of total revenue.
