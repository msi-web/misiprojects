About Me

My career started in customer support and case resolution, and over 19+ years at UScellular — a regional wireless carrier serving 4 million+ customers — that foundation grew into a specialty in policy and procedure governance, process improvement, and operations analytics. 

As a Policy & Procedure Specialist/Business Operations Analyst, I led the migration of 800+ policies and procedures to a modernized Zendesk Help Center, helped maintain a clean SOX audit record across quarterly compliance reviews, and built fraud-focused reporting that drove $7.2M in annual savings. I am at my best when I am organizing what's scattered, closing the gaps others miss, and turning both content and data into something people can actually rely on.

What drives me is keeping policy and procedure content clear, accurate, and organized so frontline teams can trust it when they need it most — paired with a process-improvement mindset that's always pushing to make things work better and more efficiently. I also lean on data and business intelligence to give that work direction: building dashboards and reports that tell the story behind the numbers, and tightening controls before small issues become expensive ones.
